Events

Rangtarang 2026 brings three days of music, dance, drama, quiz contests and a coding challenge, with exciting prizes for the winners of every event and certificates for all participants.

Guests

Our chief guests this year include a well known playback singer, a young startup founder and a national level athlete, who will inspire students with their stories and meet them after the shows.
